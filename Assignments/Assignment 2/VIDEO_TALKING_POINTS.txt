QUESTION 1: Why should/shouldn't data be scaled when performing PCA?
----------------------------------------------------------------------
ANSWER: YES, we SHOULD scale the data

REASON:
- Our features have different units and ranges
  • Hits: millions of requests
  • Entropy: 0-8 bits
  • Attack Window: seconds to hours
  • Request Size: bytes

- WITHOUT scaling: Large-range variables dominate PCA
- WITH scaling: All features get equal weight (mean=0, sd=1)
